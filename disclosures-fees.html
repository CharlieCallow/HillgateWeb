Notes on pricing

  • All fees are subject to applicable taxes. Where Dutch VAT applies, it is added at the prevailing rate.
  • We do not advertise promotional pricing and we do not offer rebates conditional on volume of unrelated services.
  • Volume-based pricing and bespoke arrangements are negotiated bilaterally and recorded in the account agreement.
  • Fees may change. Where a change is to your detriment, we will give you the notice required by your account agreement and the EMD2 framework.
