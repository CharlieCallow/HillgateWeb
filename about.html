About

A Dutch institution for regulated payments and crypto.

Hillgate Finance B.V. is an Amsterdam-based financial institution providing payment, electronic-money and crypto-asset services to institutional and professional clients across the European Economic Area.

Mission

Hillgate exists to make regulated access to payments and crypto-assets straightforward for institutional clients in the European Union. We operate a single account that brings fiat e-money, crypto custody and conversion together, governed by two licences and one consistent compliance framework.

We do not solicit retail business and we do not promise returns. We provide infrastructure.

Where we operate

We are incorporated in the Netherlands and supervised by De Nederlandsche Bank and the Autoriteit Financiële Markten. We serve eligible institutional clients in the European Economic Area on the basis of our Dutch authorisations and the passporting regimes that apply to each licence.

We do not operate as a deposit-taking bank. Client funds are safeguarded in line with the Electronic Money Directive; crypto-assets are held in segregated custody.

Regulatory posture

Our regulatory posture is conservative. Each service is offered under a specific licence, and we publish the licence on every service page. We do not market a service before it is authorised. Where authorisation is still in flight we say so explicitly, and we name the supervisor.

Full regulatory detail, including supervisor contact and dispute resolution, is on the Regulatory information page.

Leadership
