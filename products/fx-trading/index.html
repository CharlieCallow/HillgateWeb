FX Trading.

Spot conversion between supported fiat currencies, settling between e-money balances inside the institutional account. Crypto pairs are not currently offered and will be added only following MiCAR authorisation.

Provided under
Electronic Money Institution authorisation (DNB, register WFTEG R200389) MiCAR: crypto pairs commence following authorisation

What this service is

Conversion of one supported fiat currency into another, executed against a quoted rate and settled internally between e-money balances. Each conversion presents the spread and per-transaction fee pre-trade.

Supported pairs are listed in the account agreement. Crypto pairs (fiat-to-crypto, crypto-to-fiat, crypto-to-crypto) are not offered and will be added only following MiCAR authorisation. The current product is fiat-to-fiat only.

Who it is for

  • Corporate treasury functions hedging multi-currency receivables and payables.
  • Asset managers consolidating multi-currency positions inside a single institutional account.
  • Fintech partners offering currency conversion to their own onboarded counterparties under a documented programme.

No retail clients. Counterparty due diligence and ongoing monitoring apply.

How clients access the service

Both channels support FX trading. The full channel matrix and the choice environment statement are on the How it works page.

  • Web application: quote, confirm, execute, view post-trade reports.
  • API: request quote, accept quote, execute, retrieve execution reports.

Fees

FX trading is priced as a transparent spread over an indicative reference rate plus, where applicable, a per-transaction fee. Spreads are disclosed pre-trade. The full schedule is on the fees page.

Risks

FX is subject to market risk. Indicative reference rates are not guaranteed execution rates. Read the standing risk disclosure before transacting.

Regulatory basis

FX Trading is provided by Hillgate Finance B.V. under an electronic money institution authorisation issued by De Nederlandsche Bank under the Wet op het financieel toezicht, register entry WFTEG R200389.

Crypto-asset services (custody and administration, exchange, transfer services) are not yet provided. Hillgate has submitted an application for authorisation as a crypto-asset service provider under Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 to the Autoriteit Financiële Markten. Crypto pairs will be added to FX Trading only following authorisation.
