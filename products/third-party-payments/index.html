Third-Party Payments.

Outbound SEPA and SWIFT payments to third-party beneficiaries from an institutional e-money balance. The service is offered exclusively to regulated EEA institutional counterparties.

What this service is

Payment services provided under Hillgate's electronic money institution licence: outbound credit transfers instructed by the account holder to beneficiaries outside the account holder's own accounts. Funds are debited from the e-money balance held in the institutional account and credited to the beneficiary via the relevant payment scheme.

Supported channels: SEPA Credit Transfer, SEPA Instant Credit Transfer, and SWIFT. Currencies follow the supported set published in the account agreement.

Who it is for

  • Authorised investment firms, credit institutions and other supervised EEA financial counterparties.
  • Asset managers, family offices and corporate treasury functions transacting in their own name.
  • Fintech partners embedding outbound payment rails under a documented programme agreement.

No retail clients. Counterparty due diligence, sanctions screening and ongoing transaction monitoring apply.

How clients access the service

Account holders use either the web application or the Hillgate payment API. The full channel matrix and the choice environment statement, including which actions are web-app only, are on the How it works page.

  • Web application: beneficiary trusting, single and bulk payments, statements, user and permission management.
  • API: instruction of payments to previously trusted beneficiaries, transaction retrieval and reconciliation endpoints. New beneficiaries are trusted in the web application.

Safeguarding

Client funds received in connection with payment services are safeguarded under Article 7 of EMD2 as implemented in the Wet op het financieel toezicht. Safeguarding is operated through Stichting Hillgate Finance with a safeguarding account at Deutsche Bank. Safeguarded funds are kept separate from Hillgate's own funds at all times.

Fees

Payment service fees are determined per client and are documented in the account agreement. They reflect expected volume, the rails used and the operational requirements agreed during onboarding.

Risks

Payment services carry operational, technology, transfer and regulatory risk. The Risk Disclosure describes the full set and how each is managed.

Regulatory basis

Third-Party Payments are provided under the electronic money institution licence granted to Hillgate Finance B.V. by De Nederlandsche Bank (R200389). See Licences for full regulatory detail and supervisor contact addresses.
