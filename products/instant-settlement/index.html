Instant Settlement Network.

A multilateral institutional settlement network. Members move funds between each other in near-real time across the rails Hillgate operates: fiat under the electronic money institution licence, crypto-asset under the MiCAR licence.

What this service is

A network for the multilateral settlement of obligations between regulated institutional members. A member submits a settlement instruction to a counterparty also onboarded to the network. The fiat leg is debited from the instructing member's e-money balance and credited to the receiving member's e-money balance. The crypto-asset leg, where applicable, debits and credits the relevant custodied balances of each member.

Both legs settle in a single coordinated transaction recorded inside each member's institutional account.

Who it is for

  • Regulated EEA institutional counterparties already onboarded to Hillgate.
  • Members of bilateral settlement arrangements seeking a multilateral alternative.
  • Fintech partners settling institutional obligations within a documented programme.

No retail counterparties. Membership requires institutional onboarding, signed network rules and acceptance into the membership register.

How clients access the service

The full channel matrix is on the How it works page.

  • Web application: review network rules, opt in or opt out of membership, view a counterparty's published settlement instructions, instruct settlement.
  • API: instruct fiat-leg settlement to a previously trusted member, retrieve settlement history. Membership changes are web-app only.

Fees

Network membership and per-settlement fees are determined per client and are documented in the account agreement. Crypto-leg fees are aligned with the crypto-asset exchange fee structure published on the MiCAR page.

Risks

Fiat-leg settlement carries operational, technology and transfer risk. Crypto-asset-leg settlement additionally carries custody and market risk. The Risk Disclosure on the Disclosures page describes the full set.
