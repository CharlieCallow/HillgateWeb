Instant Settlement Network.

A multilateral institutional settlement network. Fiat-leg settlement is live under the EMI authorisation. Crypto-asset settlement (custody, transfer, exchange against counterparties on the network) commences following MiCAR authorisation.

Provided under
Electronic Money Institution authorisation (fiat legs, DNB register WFTEG R200389) MiCAR (crypto legs, application under assessment by the AFM)

Crypto-asset settlement is not currently offered. Until the AFM grants the MiCAR authorisation, only fiat-leg settlement between institutional members is available. See Licences.

What this service is

A network for the multilateral settlement of obligations between regulated institutional members. A member submits a payment or settlement instruction to a counterparty also onboarded to the network. The fiat leg is debited from the instructing member's e-money balance and credited to the receiving member's e-money balance.

The intended end state, available following MiCAR authorisation, is that crypto-leg settlement uses Hillgate's crypto-asset custody and transfer services to move crypto-assets between members in a single coordinated transaction.

Who it is for

  • Regulated EEA institutional counterparties already onboarded to Hillgate.
  • Members of bilateral settlement arrangements seeking a multilateral alternative.
  • Fintech partners settling institutional obligations within a documented programme.

No retail counterparties. Membership requires institutional onboarding, signed network rules and acceptance into the membership register.

How clients access the service

The full channel matrix is on the How it works page.

  • Web application: review network rules, opt in or opt out of membership, view a counterparty's published settlement instructions, instruct settlement.
  • API: instruct fiat-leg settlement to a previously trusted member, retrieve settlement history. Membership changes are web-app only.

Fees

Network membership and per-settlement fees are listed in the fees page. Crypto-leg fees will be added once the service is authorised and operational.

Risks

Fiat-leg settlement carries operational, technology and transfer risk. Crypto-leg settlement, once live, will additionally carry custody and market risk. The standing risk disclosure describes the full set.

Regulatory basis

Fiat legs are provided by Hillgate Finance B.V. under an electronic money institution authorisation issued by De Nederlandsche Bank under the Wet op het financieel toezicht, register entry WFTEG R200389.

Crypto-asset services (custody and administration, exchange, transfer services) are not yet provided. Hillgate has submitted an application for authorisation as a crypto-asset service provider under Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 to the Autoriteit Financiële Markten. Crypto-leg settlement will commence following authorisation.
