About

A regulated Dutch institution for EEA institutional counterparties.

Hillgate Finance B.V. is an Amsterdam-based financial institution providing fiat payments, currency exchange, crypto-asset exchange and settlement services to institutional counterparties under Dutch and European law.

What we do, briefly

Three institutional services under one account: Third-Party Payments, currency and crypto-asset exchange, and the Instant Settlement Network. Fiat services run under the electronic money institution licence from De Nederlandsche Bank. Crypto-asset services run under the MiCAR licence from the Autoriteit Financiële Markten.

Onboarding is mediated. There is no self-service registration. Eligibility is limited to regulated EEA institutional counterparties.

Where we operate

Hillgate is incorporated in the Netherlands. The electronic money institution licence is held with De Nederlandsche Bank. The MiCAR licence is held with the Autoriteit Financiële Markten. Cross-border activity within the EEA operates on the basis of the Dutch licences and the relevant passporting regime.

Regulatory posture

Each product page names the licence under which the service is provided. The canonical licence statement is published in the footer of every page. Full regulatory detail, supervisor contact addresses and the safeguarding arrangement are on the Licences page. The MiCAR-mandated operational policies and disclosures are on the MiCAR page.

Leadership

The Management Board is jointly responsible for the day-to-day management of Hillgate Finance B.V. The Supervisory Board oversees the Management Board on behalf of the company.
