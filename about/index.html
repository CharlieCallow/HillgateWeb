About

A regulated Dutch institution for EEA institutional counterparties.

Hillgate Finance B.V. is an Amsterdam-based financial institution providing payment, FX and settlement services to regulated EEA institutional counterparties. A MiCAR crypto-asset service provider application is under assessment by the AFM.

What we do, briefly

Three institutional products under one account: Third-Party Payments, FX Trading, and the Instant Settlement Network. Payment and FX services operate today under the EMI authorisation from De Nederlandsche Bank. Crypto-asset services will be added following authorisation under MiCAR by the Autoriteit Financiële Markten.

Onboarding is mediated. There is no self-service registration. Eligibility is limited to regulated EEA institutional counterparties.

Where we operate

Hillgate is incorporated in the Netherlands. The EMI authorisation is held with De Nederlandsche Bank. The MiCAR application is held with the Autoriteit Financiële Markten. Cross-border activity within the EEA operates on the basis of the Dutch authorisations and the relevant passporting regime.

Regulatory posture

Each product page names the licence under which the service is provided. The standing regulatory footer carries the firm-level statement on every page. No claim of MiCAR authorisation is made before the AFM grants it. Where a service is not yet available, that is said directly.

Full regulatory detail, supervisor contact addresses and the safeguarding arrangement are on the Licences page.

Leadership
