Safeguarding of client funds

Client funds received in connection with payment services are safeguarded under Article 7 of the second Electronic Money Directive as implemented in the Wet op het financieel toezicht.

Safeguarding is operated through Stichting Hillgate Finance, a foundation established for the exclusive purpose of holding safeguarded client funds. The safeguarding account is held at Deutsche Bank. Safeguarded funds are kept separate from Hillgate's own funds at all times.

Crypto-assets are not deposits. The Dutch deposit guarantee scheme does not apply to client funds safeguarded under the EMI authorisation, nor to crypto-assets that may be held in custody once the MiCAR authorisation is granted.

Anti-money-laundering supervision

Hillgate Finance B.V. is subject to the Dutch Wet ter voorkoming van witwassen en financieren van terrorisme (Wwft). The firm applies a risk-based AML and counter-terrorism financing framework, including customer due diligence, ongoing transaction monitoring, sanctions screening and suspicious-transaction reporting to FIU Netherlands. Travel-rule data exchange under Regulation (EU) 2023/1113 will apply to crypto-asset transfers once those services are authorised.
