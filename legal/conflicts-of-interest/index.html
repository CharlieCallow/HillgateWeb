Conflicts of interest.

Summary of Hillgate Finance B.V.'s framework for identifying, preventing, mitigating and disclosing conflicts of interest. The full internal policy is provided on request.

  1. Identification
  2. Prevention
  3. Mitigation
  4. Disclosure
  5. Governance and review

Identification

A register of actual and potential conflicts is maintained, covering conflicts between the firm and its counterparties, between two or more counterparties, and between employees and the firm. Categories considered include remuneration arrangements, personal holdings in crypto-assets, third-party inducements, cross-service interactions between EMI-licensed and MiCAR-licensed activities, and personal account dealing by staff. The register is reviewed at least annually and on the introduction of any new product, venue or arrangement.

Prevention

Preventive controls include segregation of duties between counterparty-facing, execution and treasury functions, restrictions on personal account dealing in crypto-assets, gift and hospitality limits, and a written policy on inducements. Compensation for staff involved in execution and treasury is not directly linked to short-term profit and loss from spreads charged to counterparties. Inducements that conflict with best execution are neither paid nor received.

Mitigation

Where a conflict cannot be prevented through structural measures, mitigation measures apply: escalation to the Conflicts Committee, additional monitoring, recusal of the conflicted individual, or independent review of the transaction.

Disclosure

Where prevention and mitigation are not sufficient to ensure with reasonable confidence that the risk of damage to a counterparty's interests is avoided, the conflict is disclosed in writing to the affected counterparty before the service is provided. The disclosure is specific enough for the counterparty to take an informed decision.

Governance and review

The Risk and Compliance function owns the Conflicts of Interest Policy. The Management Board approves the policy and any material amendments. The Supervisory Board receives an annual report on conflicts and their management. Counterparties may request a copy of the full policy at compliance@hillgate.com.
