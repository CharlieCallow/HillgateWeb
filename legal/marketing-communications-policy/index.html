Marketing communications policy.

Plain-English summary of the internal policy that governs how Hillgate Finance B.V. describes its products and services in public-facing material. The full internal policy is provided to the AFM as a private document on request.

Scope

The policy applies to all public-facing marketing communications, including this website, press releases, social media, conference material, presentations, sales decks, and emails sent to prospective counterparties before onboarding. The authenticated application is governed by the same standards, with additional requirements specific to transaction confirmations and post-trade information.

Standards

  • Clear, fair and not misleading. Every product description names the licence under which the product is provided. Forward-looking statements about MiCAR-regulated services are clearly identified as conditional on the grant of the authorisation.
  • No claim of MiCAR authorisation before grant. Communications must not imply that Hillgate is currently authorised under MiCAR. The supported phrasing is "application under assessment by the AFM" and "services will commence following authorisation". The strict wording is set out in the internal style guide and is propagated by the source-of-truth regulatory footer on every page.
  • No retail framing. Communications must not suggest that Hillgate's services are available to consumers or retail counterparties.
  • No performance, return or comparative-pricing claims. Marketing does not promise returns or favourable comparisons with competing services.
  • No promotional language. "Limited time", "join thousands of users", "exclusive offer" and similar are prohibited.
  • Risk warning visible. The persistent regulatory footer carrying the standing risk warning, the licence statement and the supervisor addresses appears on every page of this site.

Approval workflow

Marketing material is drafted by the relevant business owner, reviewed by Compliance against this policy, and signed off by the Head of Compliance before publication or external distribution. Material with a regulatory implication beyond the standing wording is also signed off by the General Counsel. A material register is maintained.

Updates following authorisation

The internal style guide includes a published checklist for the wording changes that take effect on the day MiCAR authorisation is granted. Source-of-truth components on this site (the regulatory footer and the regime banner on each product page) will be updated in one place, propagating across the public site within a single release. The legacy "application under assessment" phrasing is removed.

Requests for the full policy

The full internal policy, including the style guide, the approval workflow diagrams and the material register format, is shared with the AFM as a private document on request and is available to counterparty compliance teams at compliance@hillgate.com.
