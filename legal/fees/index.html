Notes on pricing

  • All fees are subject to applicable taxes. Where Dutch VAT applies, it is added at the prevailing rate.
  • Promotional pricing is not offered. Rebates conditional on volume of unrelated services are not offered.
  • Bespoke pricing is negotiated bilaterally and recorded in the account agreement, which prevails over this schedule.
  • Fees may change. Where a change is to a counterparty's detriment, the notice period required by the account agreement and the relevant regulatory framework applies.
  • Counterparties can read this schedule before any commitment to onboard. The fee schedule is reachable from the footer of every page.
