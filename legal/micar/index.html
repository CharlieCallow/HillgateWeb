Crypto-asset exchange fee structure

Hillgate publishes a single fee structure for its crypto-asset exchange service. This is the only published fee schedule on the site. Fiat currency exchange pricing is embedded in the rate, agreed bilaterally per client. Payment service fees are determined per client and are not published.

Indicative, pending confirmation. Numerical commercial margin values are subject to sign-off with the Hillgate trading desk before publication.

Two-component pricing

Each crypto-asset exchange is quoted at a single firm all-in price, valid for 15 seconds. The all-in price is the sum of two components:

  • Base spread. The cost of conversion attributable to the issuer or authorised distributor of the relevant electronic money token, including minting and redemption fees and directly attributable conversion costs. The base spread is identical for all clients for a given EMT.
  • Commercial margin. Agreed bilaterally with the client based on monthly and anticipated transaction volume. The commercial margin reflects Hillgate's operational cost of providing the service and a reasonable margin for the firm.

The volume-tiered structure of the commercial margin is as follows.

Volume tiers are measured per client by reference to the rolling 30-day notional value of executed crypto-asset exchange business. Bespoke pricing is documented in the account agreement, which prevails over this schedule.

Scope of application

  • Applies to the exchange of crypto-assets for funds (for example, USDC for USD, or EURC for EUR).
  • Applies to the exchange of crypto-assets for other crypto-assets. As described on the exchange page, an EMT-to-EMT exchange is executed as two independent legs (EMT to fiat, fiat to EMT). Each leg attracts the fee structure above.
  • Disclosure is in line with Article 66(4) of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 on the publication of costs and fees for crypto-asset services.
