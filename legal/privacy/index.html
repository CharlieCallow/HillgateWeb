Controller

Hillgate Finance B.V. is the controller for personal data processed in connection with this site, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Dutch Uitvoeringswet AVG.

Categories of data

  • Identification data submitted through the institutional onboarding enquiry channel (name, work email, employer, role, jurisdiction, brief description of intended use).
  • Technical data automatically collected when the site is accessed (IP address, user agent, referrer), held in standard server logs.
  • No tracking cookies, advertising identifiers or analytics cookies are used by this site at present. [TODO: confirm inventory at launch.]

Purposes and legal bases

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through the onboarding enquiry channel. Legal basis: pre-contractual steps at the request of the data subject (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent or abusive use of the site. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulator requests. Legal basis: legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Retention

Enquiry data is retained for the period necessary to respond and, where the enquiry does not result in onboarding, for a further period not exceeding 12 months for audit purposes. Server log data is retained in line with operational and security needs. Where onboarding follows, retention is governed by the account agreement and the applicable AML record-keeping requirements.

Recipients

Personal data is processed by Hillgate staff and by selected processors that support hosting, email delivery and security operations under written processing agreements. Personal data is not sold and is not shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

International transfers

Hillgate seeks to host and process personal data within the European Economic Area. Where a processor or supplier processes data outside the EEA, transfers are made under appropriate safeguards permitted by Chapter V of the GDPR, including Standard Contractual Clauses. Your rights

Data subjects have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to processing of their personal data and, where applicable, the right to data portability. Requests are submitted to privacy@hillgate.com. Data subjects also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
