Environmental disclosures.

Disclosure on the principal adverse impacts of crypto-assets on climate and other environment-related impacts, under Articles 66(5) and 67(7) of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114. Last reviewed: [TODO: date].

Status. Crypto-asset services are not currently offered. They will commence following MiCAR authorisation by the AFM. Until that point, this page sets out the firm-level statement, the methodology that will be used, and the per-asset disclosure template.

Where per-asset disclosures will appear. Per-asset environmental disclosure will be available on each crypto-asset detail page in the authenticated application, alongside the asset's other reference data, once services commence. Per-asset data is supplied by an independent third-party data provider. The template below shows the format that will be used.

Firm-level statement (Article 66(5))

Hillgate Finance B.V. does not currently provide crypto-asset services. Once the MiCAR authorisation is granted by the AFM, the firm will publish, on this page, a statement on the principal adverse impacts on climate and other environment-related adverse impacts arising from the crypto-asset services it provides at firm level.

The statement will include the consensus mechanisms of the crypto-assets serviced, attributable annualised electricity consumption, share of non-renewable energy consumed, attributable greenhouse-gas emissions, attributable water use and waste generation. Indicators will be aggregated across the assets serviced.

The statement will be reviewed at least annually and on any material change to the regulatory framework or to the underlying networks.

Methodology

The methodology follows the regulatory technical standards adopted under Article 66(5) and Article 67(7) of MiCAR. Inputs include the consensus mechanism, validator node count, geographic distribution of validators, attributable electricity consumption, attributable greenhouse-gas emissions, attributable non-renewable energy use and water use where data is available.

Per-asset data is supplied by an independent third-party data provider that is specialised in environmental impact data for crypto-assets. The operational model follows the approach used by other Dutch-supervised crypto-asset service providers. Where the underlying network does not publish reliable data, conservative proxies are used and the basis of estimation is disclosed.

Operational model

Per-asset disclosures will be retrieved from a single independent third-party data provider and rendered on each asset detail page. Aggregation at firm level will produce the firm-wide statement under Article 66(5), published on this page. The data provider's methodology is referenced in each per-asset disclosure to enable counterparties to assess the basis of the figures.

Mitigation at firm level

Hillgate does not operate validation infrastructure and does not control the consensus mechanism of the networks on which serviced crypto-assets run. Mitigation at firm level focuses on product design choices that favour lower-impact networks within the supported set, transparent per-asset disclosure, and engagement with network operators where relevant.
