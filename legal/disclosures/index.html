Disclosures.

Standing disclosures for Hillgate Finance B.V., publicly accessible without login. Each block links to the full document. The full set is reachable from the footer of every page.

Scope. These disclosures apply to services offered to regulated EEA institutional counterparties. Services are not offered to retail clients. The legal entity and the licences under which each service is offered are documented on the Licences page.

Risk warning

Crypto-assets are unregulated in many jurisdictions and their value can fall as well as rise. The value of crypto-assets can be highly volatile and counterparties may lose some or all of the funds they commit. Fiat-based services carry operational, technology, transfer and regulatory risk. FX trading is subject to market risk.

Crypto-asset services are not currently offered. They will commence following the grant of the MiCAR authorisation by the AFM. Until then, references to crypto-asset activity on this site are forward-looking.

The full standing risk disclosure is on the Risk disclosure page and is reachable from the footer of every page.

Conflicts of interest

Hillgate Finance B.V. maintains a Conflicts of Interest Policy covering the identification, prevention, mitigation and disclosure of conflicts arising from the services it provides. Compensation for execution and treasury staff is not directly linked to short-term profit and loss from spreads charged to counterparties. No inducements are paid or received that conflict with best execution.

The full policy summary is on the Conflicts of interest page. The full internal policy is provided on request.

Complaints handling

Complaints should be submitted in writing to complaints@hillgate.com or by registered post to the registered office. Counterparties are institutional. Kifid, the Dutch financial-services ombudsman service, is generally not available to institutional counterparties; the supervisor escalation route applies instead.

The full complaints handling procedure, including response timelines and the route to escalate to the supervisor, is on the Complaints page.

Environmental disclosures

Disclosure on the principal adverse impacts of crypto-assets on climate and other environment-related impacts will be made on a firm level under Article 66(5) of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114, and on a per-asset level under Article 67(7), once crypto-asset services commence following MiCAR authorisation. Per-asset data will be supplied by an independent third-party data provider; the same source will produce the disclosure on the corresponding asset detail page in the authenticated application.

The firm-level statement and a per-asset template are on the Environmental disclosures page.

Marketing communications

Hillgate maintains a Marketing Communications Policy that constrains how products and services may be described in public-facing material. The policy applies the "clear, fair and not misleading" test, names the licence under which each service is provided, and prohibits language that implies the MiCAR authorisation is in place before it is granted.

The plain-English summary is on the Marketing communications page.

Fees and pricing

The fees schedule for payments, FX trading and settlement is published on the Fees page in line with Article 66(4) of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114. Counterparties may review the schedule before any commitment to onboard.
