Applicability

The risks below apply to all services offered by Hillgate Finance B.V.: fiat payments, currency exchange, crypto-asset exchange, crypto-asset custody, crypto-asset transfers, and settlement on the Hillgate network. The text identifies which risks are specific to each service group. Counterparties should read this disclosure before any commitment to use the services and should obtain independent advice if uncertain whether a service is appropriate.

Market risk

Currency exchange rates between fiat currencies are subject to market risk. Indicative reference rates shown in the application are not guaranteed execution rates. Adverse moves between the quote, acceptance and execution windows may result in a different outcome from an indicative rate. Liquidity in less-traded pairs may be limited outside core hours.

Crypto-asset prices can be highly volatile. Counterparties may lose some or all of the funds committed to a position. Prices are influenced by supply and demand, regulatory action, technical developments in the underlying networks and macroeconomic conditions. Electronic money tokens are designed to reference a fiat currency but the reference is not a guarantee: in stressed conditions the market price of an EMT can deviate from its reference.

Custody risk

Crypto-assets held in custody on behalf of counterparties are exposed to risks distinct from those of fiat funds held under the safeguarding regime of the EMI licence. These include private-key compromise, defects in the custody stack, sub-custodian insolvency and external attack. Hillgate's custody arrangements are set out in the custody policy on the MiCAR page.

Crypto-assets are not deposits and are not covered by the Dutch deposit guarantee scheme or any equivalent compensation scheme.

Operational risk

Operational risk arises from inadequate or failed internal processes, people and systems, or from external events. Examples include failed reconciliations, mis-routed payments, identity-verification failures and third-party service interruptions. Controls are calibrated to the size and nature of the firm's activities. Residual operational risk is not zero.

Technology risk

Technology risk covers the availability and integrity of the web application, the payment API, and the third-party infrastructure on which the firm relies, including payment-scheme connectivity and the public blockchain networks used to settle crypto-asset transactions. Counterparties bear residual risk from third-party outages outside the firm's control.

Regulatory and tax risk

The regulatory framework for crypto-assets is recent. Changes to MiCAR, its delegated acts, national implementing measures or supervisory expectations may alter the scope, conditions or cost of services. The tax treatment of crypto-assets and currency exchange transactions varies by jurisdiction and by counterparty type. Hillgate does not provide tax advice. Counterparties should consult their own advisors.

Transfer risk

SEPA and SWIFT transfers are subject to the rules of the relevant payment scheme, including cut-off times, intermediary fees and recall procedures. Funds sent to an incorrect IBAN may be irrecoverable.

Crypto-asset transfers are subject to travel-rule data requirements under Regulation (EU) 2023/1113, name-matching against beneficiary records, and blockchain confirmation times. Transfers sent to an incorrect address or to a wallet on an unsupported network may be irrecoverable.
