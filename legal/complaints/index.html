Scope

The complaints procedure described below applies to expressions of dissatisfaction about the services provided by Hillgate Finance B.V. Counterparties are institutional. Retail-oriented external dispute resolution routes such as Kifid are generally not available to institutional counterparties. The supervisor route applies instead.

How to submit

Complaints should be submitted in writing by email to complaints@hillgate.com or by registered post to the registered office in Amsterdam. Complaints are accepted in English and Dutch.

What to include

  • The legal entity in respect of which the complaint is being submitted.
  • The account identifier, where applicable.
  • A clear description of the issue, including dates, transactions and counterparties involved.
  • The outcome being sought.
  • Supporting documents.

Internal escalation

If the response is not satisfactory, the complainant may escalate to the Head of Compliance at compliance@hillgate.com. The Head of Compliance reports to the Management Board and, where appropriate, to the Supervisory Board.

External escalation

For matters falling within the supervisory mandate of De Nederlandsche Bank (EMI services) or the Autoriteit Financiële Markten (MiCAR-regulated services, once applicable), complainants may contact the relevant supervisor directly via the public register on the supervisor's website.
