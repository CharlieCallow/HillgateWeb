Services · Crypto

Crypto-asset services.

Custody, transfer and execution of crypto-assets for eligible clients. Offered under our authorisation as a Crypto-Asset Service Provider from the Autoriteit Financiële Markten under the Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (EU) 2023/1114.

What this service is

Hillgate offers three crypto-asset services within the scope of MiCAR:

  • Custody and administration of crypto-assets on behalf of clients, with private keys segregated per client and held under our institutional custody arrangement.
  • Transfer services for crypto-assets, executed against client instructions with travel-rule data captured at the originator and beneficiary side.
  • Execution of orders for crypto-assets on behalf of clients, routed to vetted liquidity venues with pre-trade and post-trade controls.

We do not currently offer crypto-asset issuance, advice, portfolio management or staking.

Who it is for

  • Institutional clients requiring regulated custody of crypto-assets in the European Union.
  • Treasury and asset-management functions consolidating crypto and fiat positions in a single account.
  • Professional clients within the meaning of MiFID II Annex II, where the service is appropriate.

We do not offer this service to retail clients. The service is subject to suitability and appropriateness assessment, KYC and source-of-funds review, and ongoing transaction monitoring.

How it works

  1. Onboarding and eligibility

    You complete institutional KYC, beneficial-ownership disclosures and the MiCAR appropriateness assessment relevant to crypto-asset services.

  2. Custody set-up

    Wallets are provisioned per client. Private keys are held in a segregated, institution-grade custody environment with multi-party computation and offline backup.

  3. Fund the account

    You transfer in crypto-assets from a verified self-hosted wallet or from a regulated counterparty. Travel-rule data is captured on inbound transfers.

  4. Execute or transfer

    Orders are routed to vetted execution venues with best-execution governance. Transfers out require named-beneficiary verification.

  5. Reporting

    Position statements, transaction confirmations and execution reports are available in the platform and via API.

Fees

Custody, transfer and execution fees are published in the fees schedule. Spreads are disclosed pre-trade. We do not pay or receive inducements that conflict with best execution.
