What to include

  • Your name and the legal entity in respect of which you are complaining.
  • Your account identifier, where applicable.
  • A clear description of the issue, including dates, transactions and counterparties involved.
  • What outcome you are seeking.
  • Supporting documents or screenshots.

Response timelines

We will acknowledge receipt of your complaint within [TODO: business days] business days and provide a substantive response within the period required by the applicable regulatory framework, which differs between payment / e-money services and crypto-asset services. Where a longer period is needed, we will tell you the reason and give a revised target date. TODO: pending Q70 wording

Internal escalation

If you are not satisfied with the response, you may escalate to the Head of Compliance by writing to compliance@hillgate.example. The Head of Compliance reports to the Management Board and, where appropriate, to the Supervisory Board.

External dispute resolution

For payment and e-money matters, where the complaint is not resolved to your satisfaction, eligible clients may escalate to the Dutch financial-services ombudsman service, Klachteninstituut Financiële Dienstverlening (Kifid), subject to Kifid's eligibility rules. TODO: pending Q70 wording

Supervisor

You may also contact the relevant supervisor about Hillgate. For payment and e-money matters, that is De Nederlandsche Bank. For crypto-asset matters, that is the Autoriteit Financiële Markten. Supervisor addresses are listed in the Regulatory information disclosure.
