Hillgate Finance B.V.

Institutional payments and crypto-asset conversion, regulated in the Netherlands.

Hillgate provides fiat payments, currency exchange and crypto-asset exchange to institutional counterparties from a single Dutch-licensed entity. Fiat services run under our DNB electronic money institution licence, crypto-asset services under our MiCAR licence with the AFM. One account, one set of rails, one regulated counterparty.

What we offer

Three services for institutional treasuries and trading desks.

Payments, exchange and settlement under one institutional account. Each service names the licence under which it is provided.

Third-Party Payments

Outbound SEPA and SWIFT payments to third-party beneficiaries from a named institutional e-money balance. Initiate single or bulk payments through the web application, or through the Hillgate API for trusted beneficiaries.

Currency and crypto-asset exchange

Fiat currency exchange under the EMI licence, with pricing embedded in the rate. Crypto-asset exchange under the MiCAR licence, covering supported electronic money tokens (EURC and USDC at launch). Hillgate is the counterparty to every transaction.

Instant Settlement Network

A multilateral institutional settlement network. Members move funds between each other in near-real time across the rails Hillgate operates: fiat under the EMI licence, crypto-asset under the MiCAR licence.

Who we work with

Institutional counterparties only.

Hillgate onboards regulated entities and professional counterparties. Onboarding is mediated and is gated on documented eligibility.

  • Authorised investment firms and credit institutions in the EEA.
  • Asset managers, family offices and corporate treasury functions.
  • Fintech partners operating under a documented programme agreement.
  • Hillgate does not onboard retail clients.
Web app and API

Two channels. One account.

Clients reach Hillgate through a browser-based web application or through the Hillgate payment API. Both run on the same account and the same licences.

Web application

The primary channel. Role-based access for the institution's users, covering payment initiation, exchange quoting and execution, crypto-asset custody operations, settlement instruction, and account administration.

See how it works

Hillgate payment API

A deliberately narrow programmatic interface for payment initiation to trusted beneficiaries, exchange execution against quotes, and transaction retrieval. Manual review actions stay in the web application.
