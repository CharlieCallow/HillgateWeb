Hillgate Finance B.V.

Institutional payments and crypto-asset execution, regulated in the Netherlands.

Hillgate provides fiat payments, currency exchange, and on/off ramp services to institutional counterparties from a single Dutch-licensed entity. Fiat services run under our DNB electronic money institution licence, crypto-asset services under our MiCAR licence with the AFM. One account, one set of rails, one regulated counterparty.
