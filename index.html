Who we serve

Built for institutional and professional clients.

We do not onboard retail clients. Eligibility, suitability and source-of-funds checks apply to every account.

  • Institutional asset managers, hedge funds and family offices requiring named IBANs and crypto custody under one roof.
  • Corporate treasury teams hedging multi-currency exposure and settling supplier payments across SEPA and SWIFT.
  • Fintech partners embedding regulated payment and crypto-asset rails into their own platforms under a documented programme.
  • Professional clients within the meaning of MiFID II Annex II, where the service is offered in line with our licence scope.
