Who can transact

Regulated EEA institutional counterparties only.

Onboarding is mediated. There is no self-service registration, and no functionality is accessible to a user before onboarding is complete.

  • Authorised investment firms, credit institutions and other supervised financial entities in the EEA.
  • Asset managers, family offices and corporate treasury functions transacting in their own name.
  • Fintech partners embedding regulated payment rails under a documented programme agreement.
  • No retail clients. The fee schedule, risk disclosure and licences page are publicly readable so a prospective counterparty can review them before any commitment.
