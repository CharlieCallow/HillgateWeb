Conflicts of interest disclosure.

Hillgate Finance B.V. maintains a Conflicts of Interest Policy that covers identification, prevention, mitigation and disclosure of conflicts in connection with the services it provides. This page summarises the framework. The full policy is available on request.

Hillgate maintains a register of actual and potential conflicts arising between the firm and its clients, between two or more clients, and between employees and the firm. Sources of conflict considered include remuneration arrangements, holdings in crypto-assets, third-party inducements, cross-services interactions between EMI and CASP activities, and personal account dealing by staff.

Prevention

Preventive controls include segregation of duties between client-facing and execution functions, restrictions on personal account dealing in crypto-assets, gift and hospitality limits, and a written policy on inducements. Compensation for staff involved in execution and treasury is not directly linked to short-term P&L from spreads charged to clients.

Mitigation

Where a conflict cannot be prevented through structural measures, Hillgate applies mitigation measures, including escalation to the Conflicts Committee, additional monitoring, recusal of the conflicted individual, or independent review of the transaction. Disclosure

Where prevention and mitigation are not sufficient to ensure with reasonable confidence that the risk of damage to client interests is avoided, the conflict is disclosed to the relevant client in a durable medium before the service is provided. The disclosure is specific enough to allow the client to take an informed decision.

Governance and review

The Risk and Compliance function owns the Conflicts of Interest Policy. The Management Board approves the policy and any material amendments. The Supervisory Board receives an annual report on conflicts of interest and their management. Clients may request a copy of the full policy via our compliance contact.
