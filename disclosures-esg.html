Principal adverse impacts of crypto-assets on climate and other environment-related adverse impacts.

Scope

This disclosure covers crypto-assets in respect of which Hillgate Finance B.V. provides services under its Crypto-Asset Service Provider authorisation. It is provided on a firm level under Article 66(5) MiCAR and per-asset under Article 67(7) MiCAR. Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit.

Methodology

The methodology applied follows the criteria for principal adverse impacts on climate and other environment-related impacts set out in the regulatory technical standards under MiCAR. Inputs include consensus mechanism, validator node count, geographic distribution of validators, attributable electricity consumption, attributable greenhouse-gas emissions, attributable non-renewable energy use and water usage where data is available.

Energy and emission indicators

The mandatory indicators include annualised electricity consumption attributable to the crypto-asset, share of non-renewable energy, greenhouse-gas emissions (Scope 1, 2 and 3 where applicable), water consumption and waste generation. Indicator values are presented per crypto-asset on the relevant asset page [TODO: link or table] and updated at least annually.

Sources of impact

The principal sources of environmental impact arise from energy consumption of the consensus mechanism, hardware manufacturing and end-of-life disposal, and the energy mix of regions in which validation occurs. Proof-of-Work networks have materially different profiles to Proof-of-Stake networks; this is reflected in the per-asset disclosures.

Mitigation

Hillgate does not operate validation infrastructure and does not control the consensus mechanism of the networks on which the crypto-assets it services run. Mitigation at firm level focuses on prioritising lower-impact networks within product design, transparent per-asset disclosure to clients, and engagement with network operators where relevant.
