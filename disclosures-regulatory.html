Complaints and external dispute resolution

Clients should first submit complaints to Hillgate directly. See the Complaints disclosure for the full procedure. Where the complaint relates to payment or e-money services and is not resolved to the client's satisfaction, the client may escalate to the Dutch financial-services ombudsman service, Klachteninstituut Financiële Dienstverlening (Kifid), subject to Kifid's eligibility rules.

Safeguarding of client funds

Client funds received in connection with the EMI services are safeguarded in line with Article 7 of EMD2 as implemented in the Wft. Safeguarded funds are held in segregated accounts at supervised credit institutions and are separated from Hillgate's own funds.

Crypto-assets held in custody are not deposits and are not covered by the Dutch deposit guarantee scheme. They are held segregated per client in line with MiCAR requirements.

Anti-money laundering and counter-terrorism financing

Hillgate Finance B.V. is subject to the Dutch Wet ter voorkoming van witwassen en financieren van terrorisme (Wwft) and applies a risk-based AML/CFT framework, including customer due diligence, ongoing monitoring, sanctions screening, travel-rule compliance for crypto-asset transfers, and suspicious-transaction reporting to the FIU Netherlands.
