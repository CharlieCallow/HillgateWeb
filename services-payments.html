Services · Payments

Payments and e-money services.

SEPA and SWIFT payments, named IBANs, e-money accounts and segregated client-money handling for institutional and professional clients. Offered under our Electronic Money Institution licence from De Nederlandsche Bank.

What this service is

Hillgate offers electronic-money issuance and payment-initiation services. Eligible clients receive a named IBAN in the client's own legal name, hold balances in e-money, and send or receive transfers across SEPA Credit Transfer, SEPA Instant and SWIFT.

Client funds are safeguarded on a one-to-one basis in safeguarding accounts at supervised credit institutions, separated from Hillgate's own funds in line with Article 7 of the EMD2 as implemented in the Netherlands.

Who it is for

  • Institutional asset managers and family offices that need a named, regulated payment account.
  • Corporate treasury teams running multi-currency settlement and supplier payments.
  • Fintech partners embedding regulated payment rails under a documented programme agreement.
  • Professional clients within the meaning of MiFID II Annex II.

We do not onboard consumer or retail clients to this service. All onboarding is subject to KYC, source-of-funds and sanctions screening.

How it works

  1. Onboarding

    You submit corporate documentation, beneficial-ownership disclosures and source-of-funds evidence. We complete KYC and risk-rating before any account is opened.

  2. Account issued

    A named IBAN is issued in the client's legal name. Authorised users are configured with role-based access on the Hillgate platform.

  3. Fund the account

    You credit the IBAN by SEPA or SWIFT. Balances are held as e-money and safeguarded in segregated accounts at supervised credit institutions.

  4. Send and receive payments

    You execute SEPA Credit Transfer, SEPA Instant and SWIFT payments via the platform or API. Receipts post in real time with full audit trail.

  5. Reporting and reconciliation

    Statement exports in CAMT.053 and CSV; webhook and API endpoints for treasury and accounting reconciliation.

Regulatory basis

Payment and electronic-money services are offered by Hillgate Finance B.V. under its authorisation as an Electronic Money Institution from De Nederlandsche Bank (DNB), registration number WFTEG R200389. The service falls within the scope of the second Payment Services Directive (PSD2) and the second Electronic Money Directive (EMD2) as implemented in the Dutch Wet op het financieel toezicht (Wft).

Supervisory contact for these services: De Nederlandsche Bank, Spaklerweg 4, 1096 BA Amsterdam.

Crypto-asset services are not offered under this licence. See the Crypto page for the CASP-authorised service.
