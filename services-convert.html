Convert: FX and crypto execution.

Conversion between fiat currencies and between fiat and crypto-assets, settled directly inside your Hillgate account. Two licences, one execution flow.

What this service is

Convert lets eligible clients move value between supported currency and asset pairs in a single instruction. Two conversion directions are offered:

  • Fiat-to-fiat FX: spot conversion between supported currencies (e.g. EUR, USD, GBP) at a quoted rate, settling between two e-money balances.
  • Fiat-to-crypto and crypto-to-fiat: execution of an order on a vetted liquidity venue, with settlement against the client's fiat e-money balance and crypto-asset wallet held in custody.

Quotes are presented pre-trade. Spread and fee components are disclosed alongside the indicative reference rate.

Who it is for

  • Treasury teams hedging multi-currency exposure without leaving the Hillgate account.
  • Institutional clients funding crypto positions from fiat balances or unwinding into fiat.
  • Fintech partners offering conversion to their own eligible institutional and professional clients under a documented programme.

How it works

  1. Request a quote

    Specify the source asset, destination asset and amount. We return a firm quote valid for a short window, with the spread and fee disclosed.

  2. Confirm the conversion

    You confirm the quote inside the platform or via API. For crypto legs, pre-trade controls (limits, beneficiary checks) apply.

  3. Execution

    FX legs settle internally between e-money balances. Crypto legs route to a vetted execution venue subject to best-execution governance.

  4. Settlement

    Both sides post atomically to the relevant balance: e-money on the fiat side, custody wallet on the crypto side. A confirmation is issued immediately.

  5. Reporting

    Execution reports include the reference rate, the spread and the fee, available in the platform and via API.

Fees

Conversion is priced as a transparent spread over an indicative reference rate plus, where applicable, a per-transaction fee. The fees schedule lists both for each supported pair.

Risks

FX is subject to market risk; crypto-asset legs are additionally subject to volatility, custody and transfer risk. Indicative reference rates are not guaranteed execution rates. Read the full risk disclosure before transacting.

Regulatory basis

Convert is delivered across both of Hillgate Finance B.V.'s licences. Each leg of a conversion is offered under the licence that governs that leg:

  • The fiat leg (debit and credit of e-money balances, payment movements between accounts) is offered under the Electronic Money Institution authorisation from De Nederlandsche Bank, registration number WFTEG R200389.
  • The crypto leg (execution against a liquidity venue, transfer of crypto-assets, custody settlement) is offered under the Crypto-Asset Service Provider authorisation from the Autoriteit Financiële Markten under Regulation (EU) 2023/1114, registration number [TODO: AFM number].

Pure fiat-to-fiat FX conversions do not engage the CASP licence. Pure crypto-to-crypto executions do not engage the EMI licence. Mixed legs engage both, in the order described above.
